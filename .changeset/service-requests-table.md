Added: Service Requests has a Table view beside the Map, reached from the sidebar or from the Map/Table switch on either page. It lists every request with its number, date received, status, contact, address, intake, who took it and the details, sorts by number or date, and narrows by status and date. Every date filter also offers a This year preset.

Changed: The Service Requests Map opens on every request received this year, open and closed, rather than on open requests of any date. The dashboard's open requests count and the overview's View all link still open every open request, whenever it was received.
