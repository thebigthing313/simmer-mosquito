Changed: Chemical Applications have a Table beside the Map, reached from the sidebar or from the Map/Table switch on either page, and the switch carries every filter across, the date window included. With more than 100 chemical applications in view, the Chemical Applications map shows a summary of what is in view instead of pages of rows: how many each insecticide, application method and applicator account for, and the amount of each insecticide applied in each unit it was recorded in. Click a group to filter to it, and click it again to widen back out. Zoom in or filter to 100 or fewer and the applications are listed again, all on one page. The Product filter is now called Insecticide.
