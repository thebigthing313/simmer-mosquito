Changed: Traps have a Table beside the Map, reached from the sidebar or from the Map/Table switch on either page, and the switch carries every filter across. With more than 100 traps in view, the Traps map shows a summary of what is in view instead of pages of rows, counted by collection method and status. Click a group to filter to it, and click it again to widen back out. Zoom in or filter to 100 or fewer and the traps are listed again, all on one page.
