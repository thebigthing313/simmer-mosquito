Changed: with more than 100 samples in view, the Samples map shows a summary of what is in view instead of pages of rows, counted by status, species and non-mosquito material, with the larvae identified added up. Click a group to filter to it, and click it again to widen back out. A sample carrying two species is counted under both, so the species can add up to more than the samples in view. Zoom in, narrow the dates or filter to 100 or fewer and the samples are listed again, all on one page.
