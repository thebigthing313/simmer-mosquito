Changed: Collections have a Table beside the Map, reached from the sidebar or from the Map/Table switch on either page, and the switch carries every filter across, the date window included. With more than 100 collections in view, the Collections map shows a summary of what is in view instead of pages of rows: how many have a problem reported, how many are awaiting identification, and how many each collection method holds, with the zero results and the collected counted beside them. Click a group to filter to it, and click it again to widen back out. Zoom in or filter to 100 or fewer and the collections are listed again, all on one page.
