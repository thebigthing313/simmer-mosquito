Changed: Source Reductions have a Table beside the Map, reached from the sidebar or from the Map/Table switch on either page, and the switch carries every filter across, the date window included. With more than 100 source reductions in view, the Source Reductions map shows a summary of what is in view instead of pages of rows: how many each method and technician account for, and the sources eliminated in each unit they were recorded in. Click a group to filter to it, and click it again to widen back out. Zoom in or filter to 100 or fewer and the source reductions are listed again, all on one page.
