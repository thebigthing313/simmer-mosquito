Changed: Biocontrol Actions have a Table beside the Map, reached from the sidebar or from the Map/Table switch on either page, and the switch carries every filter across, the date window and Habitat-linked only included. With more than 100 biocontrol actions in view, the Biocontrol Actions map shows a summary of what is in view instead of pages of rows: how many each method and technician account for, how many are linked to a Habitat, and the amount released in each unit it was recorded in. Click a group to filter to it, and click it again to widen back out. Zoom in or filter to 100 or fewer and the biocontrol actions are listed again, all on one page.
