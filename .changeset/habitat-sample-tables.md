Added: Habitats and Samples each have a Table view beside the Map, reached from the sidebar or from the Map/Table switch on either page. Both tables take every filter the Map has, and the switch carries them across. Habitats list by name with type, status, access, tags and description. Samples list newest inspection first with the habitat, status, species identified and larvae counted.
