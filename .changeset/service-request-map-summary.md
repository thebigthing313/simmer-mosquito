Changed: with more than 100 service requests in view, the Service Requests map shows a summary of what is in view instead of pages of rows, counted by status, Tag and intake type, with how many days the oldest open request has waited. Click Open, Closed or a Tag to filter to it, and click it again to widen back out. A request carrying two Tags is counted under both, so the Tags can add up to more than the requests in view. Zoom in, narrow the dates or filter to 100 or fewer and the requests are listed again, all on one page.
