Changed: with more than 100 habitats in view, the Habitats map now shows a summary of what is in view instead of pages of rows: counts by habitat type, status, access and untreated. Click a group to filter to it, and click it again to widen back out. Zoom in or filter to 100 or fewer and the habitats are listed again, all on one page. Every other map list now shows 100 records a page instead of 50.
