Changed: Addresses have a Table beside the Address Book map, reached from the sidebar or from the Map/Table switch on either page, and the switch carries the search and the regions across. With more than 100 addresses in view, the Address Book map shows a summary of what is in view instead of pages of rows: how many addresses sit in each locality and under each postal code, the five largest of each and how many more. Zoom in or filter to 100 or fewer and the addresses are listed again, all on one page.
