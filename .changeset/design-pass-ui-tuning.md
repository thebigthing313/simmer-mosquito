Added: Every map page opens where you left the last one, in this browser. The first visit frames your Regions. An open service request shows how long it has waited on its row and in a new Age column on the Table, and the Map can list the oldest request first. The Daily Work group in the sidebar folds away from its heading and stays the way you left it.

Changed: Picking a service request moves the map to it and highlights its row, and the list no longer changes under you. An open request is drawn in dark yellow rather than red, because red is kept for errors, deletes and inaccessible sites. Today draws a bar for every day, weekends included, and leaves out a measure with nothing recorded this year. The Last 7 Days strip shows each change as a signed number in one colour. A new inspection starts with neither Wet nor Dry chosen and will not save until you pick one. Titles, buttons, tabs and column heads use Title Case. Page subtitles that explained your own records back to you are gone, and so is the small label above the heading on every page but a record's own. Habitats, traps and addresses sort with numbers in order, so Culvert 9 comes before Culvert 10, and so do trap, route and region names everywhere else.

Fixed: A mistyped address opens a Page Not Found inside the app with a way back, and a record that is gone says so with a link to its list. Clicking anywhere on a table row opens the record. A map still loading no longer says nothing matched. Tag chips are readable on their own colour. The trail names the Table when you opened a record from it. Averaged counts on Monthly and Annual are whole numbers with thousands grouped. Each life stage chip is read out as present or absent.
