Draw control: parts and rings

A record's shape can be made of several separated pieces, and an area piece can have a hole punched out of it. This demo runs the model we settled on, so you can feel it before it is built. Watch three things: a shape promotes to its multi form the moment it gets a second piece and demotes back the moment it loses one; a hole is cut into a piece you name first, never into whichever one you happened to click; and a hole that wanders outside its piece cannot be finished.
